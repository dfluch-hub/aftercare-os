# AFTERCARE OS — ETSY LISTING DRAFT

## Suggested title
Post Surgery Recovery Planner App | Hospital Discharge Organizer | Medication, Appointment & Caregiver Tracker | 30 Day Recovery Tool

## Opening hook
**Not another 40-page recovery printable.**

AfterCare OS is an interactive recovery organizer designed to reduce the mental load of coming home after surgery, a hospital stay, an ER visit or rehab.

Instead of giving you another blank planner to manage, AfterCare OS keeps your own medication schedule, appointments, questions, tasks and recovery notes in one calm place — then shows you the next useful organizational step.

## What’s included
- Guided “Your next step” home screen
- Daily task organizer
- User-entered medication schedule and daily log
- Appointments and questions to ask
- Morning and evening recovery check-ins
- Pain / energy / function logging
- Caregiver handoff tool
- 30-day organizational recovery journey
- Print-friendly recovery summary
- Local-first storage
- Installable web app experience
- Works across modern phone and desktop browsers

## Designed to feel simple
The app is intentionally organized into only four main areas:
**Today · Plan · Check-in · More**

No complicated health dashboard. No giant spreadsheet. No endless menu.

## Important
AfterCare OS is an organizational tool only. It does not diagnose symptoms, determine urgency, recommend treatment, prescribe medication, or replace professional medical advice.

Users should enter only information and instructions received from trusted healthcare sources.
