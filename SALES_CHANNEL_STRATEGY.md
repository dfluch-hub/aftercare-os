# AFTERCARE OS — SALES CHANNEL STRATEGY

## 1. Primary channel — own website
**Hosting:** GitHub Pages
**Checkout:** Lemon Squeezy
**Product:** AfterCare OS Complete
**Target regular price:** $19.90 one-time

Why this is the main route:
- we control branding
- no marketplace layout limitations
- software licensing fits the checkout
- Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and sales-tax/VAT mechanics as Merchant of Record
- one consistent buyer journey

### Flow
Pinterest / Google / social / recommendation
→ AfterCare sales page
→ Lemon Squeezy checkout
→ purchaser receives license key
→ AfterCare activation
→ Complete unlock

## 2. Etsy — secondary marketplace
Do NOT use Etsy as the technical licensing backbone at launch.

Initial Etsy product:
**AfterCare Recovery Companion Pack**

Suggested contents:
- 1-page “First Day Home” printable
- medication overview sheet
- questions-for-my-care-team sheet
- caregiver handoff sheet
- appointment overview
- recovery notes page
- quick-start guide

This is a real downloadable Etsy product in its own right.

Suggested price:
$6.90–$9.90

Later we can design an Etsy-specific full-app fulfillment route if it is operationally worthwhile and compliant.

Important:
Do not use Etsy listing copy/messages to move an Etsy-initiated transaction to an external checkout.

## 3. Pinterest — acquisition channel
This is likely more useful than adding another checkout platform.

Boards / pin themes:
- Post Surgery Recovery Checklist
- What to Prepare Before Surgery
- Hospital Discharge Checklist
- Recovery at Home
- Caregiver Organization
- Questions to Ask After Surgery
- Medication Organization After Hospital

Pin structure:
Problem / recognition hook
→ specific useful insight
→ AfterCare landing page

Avoid generic “Buy our app” pins.

## 4. Google / SEO — compounding acquisition
Create simple useful landing articles around real search intent:
- hospital discharge checklist
- post surgery recovery planner
- how to organize medications after surgery
- what to prepare after hospital discharge
- caregiver handoff checklist
- questions to ask at post-op follow-up

Every article can lead naturally into AfterCare.

## 5. Product Hunt — launch spike, not core business
Use once the product is polished and purchasable.

Positioning:
**AfterCare OS — the calm recovery organizer that tells you what to organize next.**

Use Product Hunt for:
- initial attention
- feedback
- backlinks
- credibility

Do not depend on it for recurring revenue.

## Channels intentionally skipped at launch

### Gumroad
Useful platform, but duplicative once Lemon Squeezy handles the primary checkout.
Current direct-sale fees are materially higher than Lemon Squeezy's base fee.

### Payhip
Reasonable backup platform, but also duplicates checkout/product management.
Keep it as contingency rather than launching another storefront.

### Shopify
Unnecessary monthly overhead and complexity at this stage.

### App Store / Google Play
Not needed for the PWA launch.
Revisit only if native-store discovery becomes strategically valuable.

## Launch order

1. GitHub Pages live
2. Lemon Squeezy product + licensing
3. production test purchase
4. privacy / terms / refund pages
5. sales-page screenshots
6. Pinterest content
7. Etsy Companion Pack
8. SEO pages
9. Product Hunt launch

Do not reverse this order and create marketing before the purchase flow works.
